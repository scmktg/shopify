## Choosing a water filter in Sydney

For drinking water at a single kitchen tap, compare [under-sink water filters](/water-filters/under-sink) and [reverse osmosis systems](/water-filters/reverse-osmosis). For treatment before water is distributed through the property, compare [whole-house water filter systems](/water-filters/whole-house).

The right system depends on the water source, treatment goal, available space and required flow rate. We keep product pages focused on the exact system specifications rather than using the Sydney location page as a substitute for product information.

## Whole-house installation in Sydney

The standard whole-house installation package is **$2,399 inc GST supplied and installed**, subject to confirmed Sydney service coverage and standard installation conditions.

The service is intended for suitable mains-water properties where sediment and activated-carbon treatment is required before water reaches multiple outlets. Installation is normally on the incoming cold-water main where the plumbing layout makes that appropriate.
