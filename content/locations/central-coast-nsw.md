## Choosing a Central Coast water filtration system

For drinking water at the kitchen, compare [under-sink water filters](/water-filters/under-sink) and [reverse osmosis systems](/water-filters/reverse-osmosis). For filtration at every tap and shower, compare [whole-house water filter systems](/water-filters/whole-house).

Central Coast households generally have three clear paths:

- **Under-sink filtration** for drinking water at one tap.
- **Reverse osmosis** where membrane filtration is required at the drinking tap.
- **Whole-house filtration** for sediment and activated-carbon treatment before water reaches multiple outlets.

## Water on the Central Coast

Most of the Central Coast is on mains water supplied by Central Coast Council. Common filtration goals include improving chlorine taste and odour and managing sediment. A carbon under-sink filter can treat the kitchen drinking tap, while whole-house systems treat water before it reaches multiple outlets.

Properties on rural blocks may draw drinking water from rainwater tanks. Tank water needs a different treatment approach and can require sediment removal, carbon treatment and an appropriate disinfection stage such as UV. See the [rural tank-water guide](/use/rural-tank-water) for that use case.
