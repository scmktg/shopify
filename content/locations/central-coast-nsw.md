## Water on the Central Coast

Most of the Central Coast is on mains water supplied by Central Coast Council. The water meets the Australian Drinking Water Guidelines and is disinfected before distribution.

The most common local filtration goals are improving chlorine taste and odour and managing occasional sediment. A carbon under-sink filter can treat the kitchen drinking tap, while whole-house systems treat water before it reaches multiple outlets.

Properties on rural blocks often draw drinking water from rainwater tanks. Tank water needs a different treatment approach, typically combining sediment removal, carbon treatment and an appropriate disinfection stage such as UV. See the [rural tank-water guide](/use/rural-tank-water) for that use case.
