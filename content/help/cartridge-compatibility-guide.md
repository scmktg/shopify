Which cartridge fits which system, in one page. Three Australian housing standards cover almost every residential and commercial water filter sold here. Get the size right and the rest is straightforward.

## The three sizes that matter

Every residential cartridge in this catalogue belongs to one of three size standards. Measure once, buy with confidence.

- **Standard 10" × 2.5"** — the most common Australian residential size. About 9.75 inches tall (the "10 inch" label is nominal) and 2.5 inches wide. Suits drinking-water under-sink systems, low-flow single-stage filters, and RO pre/post stages. Connection: double open-end (DOE) unless noted.
- **Big Blue 10" × 4.5"** — same height as standard, wider body. Holds roughly five times more media than a 10" × 2.5". Common on smaller whole-house systems and holiday-home installs.
- **Big Blue 20" × 4.5"** — twice the height again. The whole-house workhorse for four-bedroom Australian homes — long cartridge life, low pressure drop, comfortable flow when multiple outlets run at once. The reference size for "whole-house" cartridges throughout this site.

Two less-common sizes show up occasionally: **20" × 2.5"** (tall and narrow — some legacy under-sink systems) and **specialty form factors** with built-in O-rings (typically labelled 222 or 226 — not interchangeable with DOE cartridges). Both are flagged on the relevant product pages.

## Common 3-stage Big Blue 20" × 4.5" — whole-house

The dominant whole-house configuration in Australia. Service interval listed against typical four-bedroom town-water use; halve the sediment interval on tank or bore supplies.

| Stage | Cartridge | Micron | Replace |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — Sediment | Pleated polypropylene | 5 µm | 6–12 months |
| 2 — Carbon | Carbon block (CTO) | 5 µm | 6–12 months |
| 3 — Polish | Granular carbon (GAC) **or** catalytic carbon for chloramine | 5 µm | 12 months |

Tank-water variant: bump stage 1 to a 20 µm pleated pre-filter and add a second 5 µm pleated downstream. UV-equipped three-stage variants replace stage 3 with the UV chamber — UV-lamp replacement is annual regardless of throughput.

## Common 3-stage standard 10" × 2.5" — under-sink

The under-sink equivalent — same logic at smaller scale.

| Stage | Cartridge | Micron | Replace |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — Sediment | Polypropylene spun (PP) | 5 µm | 6–12 months |
| 2 — Carbon | Carbon block (CTO) | 5 µm | 6–12 months |
| 3 — Polish | Granular carbon (GAC) or T33 post-carbon | 1–5 µm | 12 months |

Some 3-stage under-sink systems substitute stage 3 with a specialty cartridge — alkaline mineral, fluoride-removal activated alumina, or ultrafiltration. Treat the housing as a slot the cartridge has to fit physically (10" × 2.5" DOE) and chemically (compatible with the upstream stages).

## Common 5- and 6-stage reverse osmosis — under-sink

RO systems use the standard 10" × 2.5" housing for pre- and post-filters, plus a smaller dedicated housing for the membrane.

| Stage | Cartridge | Replace |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Sediment | 5 µm PP spun | 6–12 months |
| 2 — Carbon block | 5 µm CTO | 6–12 months |
| 3 — Granular carbon | 5 µm GAC | 6–12 months |
| 4 — RO membrane | 50, 75, or 100 GPD TFC | 2–3 years |
| 5 — Post-carbon (T33) | Inline polishing carbon | 12 months |
| 6 — *(optional)* Alkaline / remineraliser | Inline mineral cartridge | 12 months |

The 5- vs 6-stage decision is whether you want a remineralisation stage downstream of the membrane (stage 6). RO strips minerals along with contaminants — some buyers want the calcium and magnesium added back, some don't. Either way, the membrane and pre-filter set are identical.

## Micron-rating quick guide

Lower micron = finer filtration = faster pressure drop. Match the rating to the job, don't over-filter.

- **20 µm** — coarse pre-filter for rural tank water and very dirty supplies. Long life, minimal pressure drop. Useless on its own as a drinking-water filter.
- **5 µm** — the Australian residential standard. Catches visible grit, rust, and most particulates without choking flow. Almost always the right pick for stage 1.
- **1 µm** — fine polishing stage only. Pair it downstream of a 5 µm or 20 µm pre-filter; running 1 µm as the only sediment stage will clog inside weeks on most supplies.
- **0.5 µm and finer** — carbon-block territory, typically achieving fine sediment filtration as a side effect of contact-time-optimised carbon. Cyst reduction (Cryptosporidium, Giardia) generally requires 1 µm absolute.

Micron ratings can be nominal (the cartridge captures most particles at that size) or absolute (the cartridge captures essentially all particles at that size). Every product page lists which.

## What fits what — the short version

- **Whole-house Big Blue 20" × 4.5"** → sediment pleated 5 µm + carbon block 5 µm + (catalytic carbon OR UV OR GAC) — see the table above.
- **Whole-house Big Blue 10" × 4.5"** → same layout, smaller cartridges, halve cartridge life.
- **Under-sink 3-stage standard 10" × 2.5"** → PP spun 5 µm + carbon block 5 µm + GAC or T33.
- **Under-sink RO 5/6-stage** → PP spun + carbon block + GAC + TFC membrane + T33 (+ optional alkaline).
- **Bench-top single-stage** → typically a proprietary cartridge — check the product page; not cross-compatible with housing-based systems.
