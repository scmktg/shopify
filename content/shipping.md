We dispatch orders Monday to Friday from our Central Coast NSW warehouse. Tracked shipping on every order, with no signature required by default.

Shipping rates are calculated at checkout based on the delivery address. Bulky items (tanks, large whole-house systems) may attract a freight surcharge — this is shown at checkout before payment.

## Dispatch times

- Orders placed **before 1pm AEST** on a business day → ship the same business day.
- Orders placed **after 1pm AEST**, on weekends, or on Australian public holidays → ship the next business day.

You will receive a confirmation email when the order is placed, and a second email with the tracking link when the parcel is collected by the carrier.

## Delivery times

Approximate transit times once the parcel has been collected:

- **Sydney metro / Central Coast** — 1–2 business days
- **Newcastle, Hunter, Wollongong** — 1–2 business days
- **Other capital cities (Brisbane, Melbourne, Adelaide, Hobart, Canberra)** — 2–4 business days
- **Perth and Darwin** — 4–7 business days
- **Regional and remote addresses** — 3–10 business days, depending on the carrier's network

These are carrier estimates, not guarantees. Christmas, Easter, and other peak periods can add 1–3 business days. Tracking is the most accurate source once the parcel has shipped.

## Local pickup

Local pickup from our Central Coast NSW warehouse is available on request. Contact us before placing the order to arrange — we will confirm pickup hours and address details. Pickup orders are processed on the same dispatch schedule as shipped orders.

## Damaged or missing parcels

If a parcel arrives damaged, photograph the packaging and contents before unpacking further and contact us within 48 hours. If a parcel is marked delivered but you cannot locate it, check with neighbours and authorised receivers first; if you still cannot find it, contact us with your order number and we will lodge a trace with the carrier. Most "missing" parcels turn up within a few days.
