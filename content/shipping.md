## Shipping prices

**Your shipping cost is calculated at checkout**, based on:

- **Parcel size** — the size of your packaged items.
- **Delivery location** — your postcode and whether it is metro, regional or remote.

You'll see the delivery charge **before you pay**. Selected products include **free shipping**; oversized or specialist deliveries may require a freight quote.

## Our delivery partners

We choose the fastest and most economical suitable carrier for each order. Most parcels are delivered by Aramex or Australia Post. Larger items may travel with specialist couriers including Go Logistics, FedEx and TNT.
