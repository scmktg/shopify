Enviro Aqua is an Australian-owned water filtration specialist based in Wyong on the NSW Central Coast. We sell water filters, cartridges, and full filtration systems direct to homeowners, tradies, and businesses across Australia at wholesale prices.

## What we do

We specialise. Water filtration is what we sell — under-sink and bench-top filters, whole-house systems, reverse osmosis, UV sterilisers, replacement cartridges, pumps, dosing tanks, and the plumbing fittings that make the systems install cleanly. We do not branch into general bathroom retail, pool supplies, or aquariums. The catalogue is deliberately narrow because the customers who buy from us want the people on the other end to know what they are talking about.

## Why we sell direct

Most Australian water filter retail goes through layers — distributor, wholesaler, retailer, installer, customer. Every layer adds margin. We sell direct from the warehouse to the buyer at wholesale price, so the same product reaches a homeowner for less than they would pay at a chain plumbing-supply store, and reaches a tradie for the same price (without the trade-account paperwork).

## One price for everyone

There is no separate trade portal on this site. There are no quotes. No "call for pricing". No login wall hiding the real price. The price you see on the page is the price every customer pays — homeowner, tradie, commercial, or repeat installer.

This is not a gimmick. It is the reason the business exists. The traditional model treats price as a negotiation; we treat it as a fact. That makes ordering faster for everyone and removes the part of the buying experience most people dislike.

We have a real showroom and warehouse at 6/45 Amsterdam Cct, Wyong NSW 2259. Local customers are welcome to visit during business hours (Mon–Fri 9am–5pm AEST), or use Click & Collect — orders are usually ready within two hours during business hours.
