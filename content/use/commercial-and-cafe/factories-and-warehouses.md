A **factory or warehouse water bubbler** is a commercial drinking-water station installed where workers can access water during shifts and breaks. For industrial sites, the biggest differences from an office are **heat, dust, exposure, shift peaks, physical wear and the distance between work zones**.

Safe Work Australia guidance says workers should have access to clean, safe drinking water and notes that drinking fountains are one way water can be supplied hygienically. It also recommends drinking water be at or below **24 °C**; individual jurisdictions determine how model WHS guidance applies locally.

Enviro Aqua's [commercial water bubbler range](/commercial-water-bubblers) consists of three filtered, chilled models. **All three are rated at 20 L/hr and deliver chilled water at approximately 8–12 °C.** All three are WaterMark certified under **Certificate 023484**.

## Factories: plan for peak demand

Factories often have concentrated drinking-water demand at shift changes, scheduled breaks and after physically demanding work. That means average daily usage is less useful than **how many people may need water in the same 10–20 minute window**.

All three Enviro Aqua commercial models are rated at **20 L/hr chilled output at approximately 8–12 °C**. On a larger site, two or more stations can distribute demand and reduce the distance workers need to travel from their work area.

## Warehouses: bottle filling is often important

Warehouse workers frequently carry bottles onto the floor, so a separate filling outlet can be more useful than a bubbler spout alone.

The **YL-600C Water Bubbler Square** and **YL-600P Water Bubbler Grey** include a side tap for bottles or cups. The **YL-600R Water Bubbler Round** is the simpler direct-drinking option.

For sites with forklifts, picking zones or long aisles, placement matters as much as the model. Drinking water should be accessible without creating unnecessary travel through vehicle routes or restricted work areas.

## Indoor vs outdoor industrial sites

Use the cabinet construction to match the environment:

- **SUS304 stainless steel:** suitable for indoor and sheltered commercial positions.
- **HDPE polymer cabinet:** rust-free and UV-stable for exposed outdoor positions.

For loading docks, open yards, outdoor workshops or exposed factory entrances, the [HDPE commercial bubbler](/bubblers-and-coolers/bubblers/commercial-rust-free-filtered-cold-water-bubbler-wm) is the intended model from this range.

## Drinking water in hot workplaces

Hot factories, workshops and warehouses can increase drinking-water demand substantially. Safe Work Australia guidance recommends access to drinking water and indicates water should be cool, with **24 °C or below** given as guidance.

All three Enviro Aqua commercial models chill water to approximately **8–12 °C**. Sites with unusually high heat load or large crews should consider multiple drinking points and review their broader heat-management and hydration procedures with the relevant WHS guidance.

## Filtration in industrial environments

All three commercial bubbler models include drinking-water filtration. The **YL-600C square model** uses sediment plus activated carbon, the **YL-600P HDPE model** uses PP sediment plus activated carbon, and the **YL-600R round model** uses an integrated drinking-water cartridge.

Dusty or high-use industrial environments should be placed on a planned inspection and filter-change schedule.

## WaterMark and installation

WaterMark is mandatory for certain plumbing and drainage product categories identified by the WaterMark Schedule of Products. Not every mains-connected product is automatically subject to the scheme, so the relevant product category and installation need to be checked.

All three Enviro Aqua commercial bubbler models are WaterMark certified under **Certificate 023484**.

Use an appropriately licensed plumbing practitioner for the mains-water connection and any drainage work, and confirm site-specific plumbing and WHS requirements before installation.

## Where should a factory or warehouse place drinking stations?

Common locations include staff break and lunch areas, warehouse picking zones, production-floor entrances, loading areas and dispatch zones, workshop entrances, outdoor work yards and locations near high-heat processes where safe and appropriate.

Avoid placing the only drinking station in an office area that is inconvenient for floor workers. On large sites, multiple smaller stations can provide better practical access than a single central unit.
