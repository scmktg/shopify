Every Enviro Aqua order ships with tracked delivery. The tracking link goes out by email when the carrier collects your parcel — usually the same business day for orders placed before 12pm AEST.

## How to track your order

1. **Check your email** for a message from [info@enviroaqua.com.au](mailto:info@enviroaqua.com.au) with the subject line "Your order is on its way". This is sent the moment the carrier scans the parcel out of our Wyong NSW warehouse.
2. **Click the tracking link** in that email. It opens the carrier's tracking page (Australia Post, Aramex, CouriersPlease, or Allied Express depending on the shipping tier) with the latest scan, per-leg ETA, and a delivery date estimate.
3. **Bookmark the link** if you want to check progress over the next few days — the carrier updates it as the parcel moves between depots.

If you cannot find the email, check your spam, promotions, or filtered folders before assuming it has not been sent.

## Can't find the tracking email?

A few common reasons:

- **The order hasn't shipped yet.** Orders placed before 12pm AEST on a business day ship the same day; orders placed after 12pm, on weekends, or on Australian public holidays ship the next business day.
- **The email is in spam or promotions.** Search your inbox for "enviroaqua" or "tracking".
- **The address on the order has a typo.** If your order confirmation went to the wrong email, the tracking email will too.

If it has been more than one business day since you ordered and the tracking email still has not arrived, get in touch and we will resend it.

- **Phone:** [(02) 8772 8162](tel:+61287728162) (Mon–Fri 9am – 5pm AEST)
- **Email:** [info@enviroaqua.com.au](mailto:info@enviroaqua.com.au) — include your order number if you have it

## Click & Collect orders

Click & Collect from our Wyong NSW showroom does not use a carrier tracking link. Instead, we email or text you when your order is ready for collection — usually within two hours of order placement during business hours. Bring the ready-for-collection email and photo ID when you visit:

- **Address:** 6/45 Amsterdam Cct, Wyong, NSW 2259
- **Hours:** Monday to Friday, 9am – 5pm AEST

## Pallet freight orders

Commercial RO plants, 50L+ pressure tanks, and dosing tanks ship by pallet through Allied Express. The carrier will phone you to arrange a delivery window once the pallet reaches your destination depot — make sure the phone number on your order is one you can answer during business hours.

## Something gone wrong?

For damaged parcels, missing parcels, or "delivered" parcels you cannot locate, see our [shipping page](/shipping/) and [returns page](/returns/) for the steps to follow, or contact us directly. We respond to all enquiries within one business day.
